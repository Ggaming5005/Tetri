# Original Tetri artwork

`mobile/assets/illustrations/welcome.png` is an unused design exploration generated with the built-in image generation tool. It has a transparent background and uses no real bank's branding, but repeated edits retained shading that does not satisfy the flat design rule. It is kept for provenance and is not bundled in the app.

The app uses its own wordmark and crisp, code-drawn GEL, USD and EUR flag circles. These render consistently across Android and widget golden tests without emoji font dependencies. The Inter files in `mobile/assets/fonts/` are Google Fonts assets distributed under the accompanying OFL license.

Generation prompt:

> Create a restrained premium editorial flat illustration for Tetri, a fictional sandbox multicurrency money app: three abstract rounded coin-like forms in an open architectural arrangement, connected by one fine continuous curved line suggesting money moving between three currencies. Use generous negative space, quiet asymmetry, precise edges, cobalt blue #3B4BF5, pale gray #E7E5E4 and #F5F5F4, and charcoal #0C0A09 linework. Transparent background. No text, letters, currency symbols, real bank logos, credit cards, people, gradients, shadows, glow or 3D rendering. Horizontal composition for a 300 by 180 logical pixel mobile image.

Final edit prompt:

> Preserve the exact composition of the three shapes and connecting thin curve and preserve the transparent background. Make the style rigorously flat: replace shading, texture, grain and tonal variation with solid #3B4BF5, #E7E5E4 and #F5F5F4 fills. Remove stray specks and fringes outside the silhouettes. Smooth clean edges and uniform charcoal #0C0A09 linework. No gradients, shadows, texture or text.
